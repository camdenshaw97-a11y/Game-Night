Game Night

Pick a box. One screen shows the board, and everyone plays from their phone.

    How a night works

    1. Open this page on the computer plugged into the TV and pick a game.
    2. The TV shows a QR code and a four-letter room code.
    3. Everyone scans it with their phone. No app, no account.

    Games nap when nobody is playing. The first person in waits up to a minute while one wakes up, and after that it is quick for everybody.
